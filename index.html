Questions

Good to know

How long does a website take?

Most one-page sites take about a week, larger builds two to three — mostly depending on how fast you get me your text and photos.

What if I don't like the design?

You only pay the deposit after you've seen the rough design and you're happy with it.

What happens if I stop paying the monthly fee?

It runs only while the fee is paid — cancel and I stop hosting and supporting the site. If you paid for the build upfront, I hand over your files so you can host it elsewhere.
