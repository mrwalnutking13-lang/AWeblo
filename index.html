Questions

Good to know

How long does a website take?

Most one-page sites take about a week; larger builds two to three. It mostly depends on how fast you get me your text and photos.

What if I don't like the design?

You only pay the deposit after you've seen the rough design and you're happy with it. You're never paying for something you haven't seen.

What happens if I stop paying the monthly fee?

Hosting, maintenance and support run only while the monthly fee is paid. If you cancel, I stop paying for your hosting and turn off support. If you paid for the build upfront, I hand over your site files so you can host it somewhere else.
