Questions

Good to know

How long does a website take?

Most one-page sites are done in about a week, and larger multi-page builds usually take two to three. It really comes down to how quickly you can get me your content — text, photos and logos.

What do you need from me to start?

Just a rough idea of what you want and a few examples of sites you like. If you already have a logo, photos or written content, send those too — if you don't, I'll work with what you've got and we'll fill in the gaps.

Why is the price a range instead of a flat rate?

Because a simple one-page site and a full custom build aren't the same amount of work. Price depends on the size and complexity of the website. You'll get one clear number before anything starts — no hourly surprises.

What happens to the deposit if I don't like the design?

You only pay the deposit after you've seen the rough design and you're happy with the direction. That's the whole point of doing it this way — you're never paying for something you haven't seen.

Do I have to buy the monthly maintenance?

No — it's completely optional. Plenty of clients launch and handle things themselves. Maintenance is there if you'd rather have someone keeping the site updated and fixed without thinking about it.

Can you add a feature that isn't listed?

Almost always, yes. Booking systems, payment links, quote calculators, galleries, integrations with tools you already use — describe what you want it to do and I'll tell you straight whether it's doable and what it adds to the price.
